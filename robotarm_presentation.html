Sampling Flow (取樣)

●Step 1POST /vision/reset — clear previous detection data
●Step 2POST /vision/trigger — robot executes the vision TMFlow node
●Step 3Poll GET /vision/latest every 500 ms until results arrive
●Step 4Map pixel coordinates → tray cell numbers via Homography transform
●Step 5GET /camera/capture — capture image and display on screen

Core Feature

Pick-and-Place Execution Flow

Steps per Selected Cell

●1. Move to cellRobot PTP to cell coordinates X,Y,Z (lowered by 80 mm for gripping)
●2. Pick objectClose gripper, lift up — QueueTag tracks step completion
●3. InspectMove to POS_INSPECT (275, 0, 400 mm)
●4. WaitHold at inspection position for 10 seconds
●5. Place backReturn object to its original cell position
●6. RepeatContinue with next cell in the queue

Special Positions

●POS_STANDBYX=0, Y=300, Z=500 mm — safe waiting position
●POS_INSPECTX=275, Y=0, Z=400 mm — quality inspection position
●LOWER_MM = 80Extra 80 mm descent below cell coordinate to grip the object

Flow Control

●QueueTag pollingBackend polls every 100 ms with 60 s timeout — tracks each step completion
●Mid-sequence pauseExecution loop yields when robotPaused = true
●Emergency stopStopAndClearBuffer(0) sent immediately to robot
